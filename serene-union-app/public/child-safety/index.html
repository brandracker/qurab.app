Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026 · Qurb: Find Your Deen Mate

Zero-Tolerance Commitment: Qurb maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE). We are committed to safeguarding minors through strict age gating, proactive moderation, user reporting, and swift collaboration with legal and law enforcement authorities.

1. Strict Age Gating (Adults 18+ Only)

Qurb is exclusively dedicated to lawful Islamic matrimony (Nikah) between consenting adults. In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global child protection standards:

  • You must be at least 18 years of age (or legal majority in your country) to create an account or access the service.
  • Minors are strictly prohibited from using Qurb. Any account created by or depicting a minor under 18 will be permanently terminated immediately upon detection.

2. Absolute Prohibition on CSAE / CSAM

We strictly prohibit any depiction, discussion, promotion, solicitation, or transmission of:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in any form (photographs, audio, text, or external links).
  • Grooming behaviors, predatory communication, or attempts to exploit minors.
  • Inappropriate imagery, sexualized content, or child endangerment of any kind.

3. In-App Reporting & Detection Mechanisms

We empower our community and employ comprehensive safeguards to protect children and detect violations:

  • 1-Tap In-App Reporting: Every profile and chat screen features an easily accessible "Report" button allowing users to report suspicious content or underage users directly to our dedicated Trust & Safety team.
  • Automated & Human Review: Flagged content and accounts are prioritized for immediate review by human moderators.
  • Account Freezing & Expulsion: Any account suspected of involvement in CSAE/CSAM is immediately suspended, and all associated media is quarantined.

4. Reporting to Authorities (NCMEC & Law Enforcement)

In accordance with all applicable regional and international child protection laws:

  • Qurb immediately reports any instances of CSAM/CSAE to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and competent law enforcement agencies.
  • We preserve digital evidence and cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations to ensure perpetrators are held accountable under the law.
